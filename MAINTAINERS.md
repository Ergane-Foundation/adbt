## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests; every change needs a maintainer's approval.
- Triage issues and label them.
- Decide on releases, keyboard shortcuts, and changes to how adbt talks to `adb`.
- Handle security reports privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and
  enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How decisions are made

Small changes are decided in the pull request. Larger changes (new screens,
changed shortcuts, new dependencies) start as an issue so others can comment;
the lead maintainer makes the final call and records the reason in the issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who make several solid pull requests and help others in reviews and
issues may be invited to become maintainers.
