# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** open a GitHub Discussion (Q&A or Ideas). Please search
  first; your question may already be answered.
- **Bugs:** open an issue. Include your adbt version (`adbt --version`), operating
  system, phone model and Android version, and the steps to reproduce.
- **Documentation problems:** open an issue, or a pull request against `website/docs`.
- **Security problems:** do not post them publicly; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, check:

- [README.md](README.md) and the [documentation website](https://adbt-tui.vercel.app);
- the [troubleshooting guide](https://adbt-tui.vercel.app/docs/troubleshooting);
- that `adb devices` lists your phone as `device`, not `unauthorized` or
  `offline` (most "no device" problems come from USB debugging or authorization).

This project is maintained by volunteers, so answers can take a few days. There
is no paid support.
